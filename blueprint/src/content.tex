\chapter{Mathematical background}
\label{chap:mathematical-setting}

This chapter fixes the stable homotopy, Steenrod, Ext, Adams-tower, and
convergence language used throughout the formalization, including the general
tower and page constructions used by later chapters. The target-specific
class and its permanence statement are introduced with the final proof rather
than treated as part of the general background. Detailed algebraic and
internal spectral-sequence foundations and their low-level auxiliary lemmas are retained
in Appendix~\ref{chap:mathematical-foundations-appendix}.

Parameterized constructions retain their stated categorical, comparison, and
convergence hypotheses. Their formalization does not construct the fixed
sphere or synthetic model: the existence and applicability of those instances,
and the literature and computation deliveries on them, remain separate
mathematical obligations.

suspension autoequivalence are proved below. The original stable cubical quotient now has constructed abelian
group operations and functorial maps, with addition equal to actual loop
concatenation at every stage; the fixed coefficient identification is additive and has a unique normalized
nonzero map from the ordinary sphere. Finite pointwise products give actual
products in the ordinary source localization, with the expected abelian stable
homotopy groups. Actual path concatenation and the loop equivalence construct
preadditivity and finite biproducts on both the ordinary source and its local
subcategory, preserving the original zero morphisms. Both now have coherent
additive integer shifts from the original signed suspension iterates. Actual compact-open loops give the strict and
localized suspension--loop adjunction and an additive stable degree shift.
Actual homotopy fibers now have natural all-integer stable long exact sequences,
which detect stable equivalences and prove fiber finiteness and lower bounds.
Actual mapping-path lifts and strict kernel comparisons prove that path
homotopy pullback preserves stable equivalences. Actual homotopy right fractions
and strict roofs now give fiber exactness on all represented source hom groups
and stable-homotopy detection of arbitrary source isomorphisms. Actual fiber
iteration and signed rotation give all three Hom-exact positions, and actual
homotopy squares give compatible fiber maps. Actual free-cell replacements have proved strict lifting, including homotopies
with both endpoints fixed. Strict homotopy classes have actual small coproducts;
their specified stable source coproducts now have proved universal properties.
Actual strict sequential telescopes recover the colimits of the original
stable homotopy groups. Compact representatives prove that these same group
functors preserve all small source coproducts. Actual cofibrant cone exactness
and HEP stable excision are also proved. The original free cubical generators
have levelwise HEP, and their actual replacements are well based. The specified
Milnor-to-geometric telescope comparison is an isomorphism; original stable
classes have finite-stage detection. Actual upper sphere covers give cofiber
steps that kill the specified upper degrees and preserve the lower degrees.
Their actual telescopes construct the Postnikov truncation functors and units;

HF2 object; its injectivity still requires the mod-two sphere degree theorem.
Every original positive-dimensional sphere map now has an actual finite
collapse decomposition and the corresponding stable sum; the local branch

Actual closed HEP supplies neighborhood deformation data and full two-end
cylinder extension. The specified topological telescope comparison is a
homotopy equivalence for actual closed HEP sequences. Original free cubical
generators have ordinary cube-cell attachments in every level, and the actual
cube boundary pairs agree with finite order-cone pairs. Actual cell refinement
gives ordinary cube attachments at every replacement stage. Their finite-order
models retain both original old-space comparisons. Recursive order models and
actual telescope invariance give a CW realization mapping by a specified
homotopy equivalence to every original replacement level. Actual relative
rectification fixes both basepoints throughout the inverse homotopies, proving
based CW type and the original good-replacement theorem. Cubical
cofibrations have derived levelwise HEP, and the actual mapping cylinder gives
a cofibration--stable-equivalence factorization for cofibrant prespectra.
The actual signed fiber triangles
have proved square completion for arbitrary source morphisms, and their
isomorphism closure gives the actual pretriangulated structure. The actual
composite-fiber fourth triangle and its two face maps prove the octahedron
axiom, giving the actual triangulated structure. The original stable homotopy
functors are homological for these triangles. Actual local objects are closed
under fibers and cofibers, and the existing local category is triangulated.
The stable homotopy functors and the original sphere representation preserve
the constructed addition. Singular
realization supplies functorial good prespectrum levels with strict evaluation,
exact lifting of cubical loops, stable surjectivity, and Kan fillers from
explicit geometric horn retractions. Unique positive flag coordinates also

subdivision has the corresponding actual homeomorphism, and iterated chain
subdivision has a proved geometric mesh-decay bound. Actual iterated
homeomorphisms, open-star contractions and Lebesgue refinement give star
assignments, relative based simplicial approximation and entire-simplex
boundary models for finite order complexes. Singular evaluation reflects
nullhomotopies of continuous maps factoring through such actual complexes.
Actual order mapping-cone pushouts and relative singular gluing are available
for the remaining finite-cell comparison, including gluing that retains both
prescribed ends and actual cone homeomorphisms preserving arbitrarily
subdivided simplex boundaries. Compact maps and relative homotopies in
closed-embedding sequential colimits factor through actual finite stages;
the original cubical homotopy sets preserve these colimits. The first actual
spectrification step has a strict coherent unit proved to be a stable equivalence.
Actual sequential prespectrum colimits are constructed; stable homotopy
preserves their closed-embedding unions. Reduced mapping cylinders give an
actual closed spectrification telescope with stable-equivalence unit; its
all-dimensional stabilization bijections are proved for T1 input levels.
Actual free-cubical prespectrum attachments now give a functorial T1
replacement with a natural levelwise weak equivalence and proved based CW type. Arbitrary products of the actual stabilized telescopes have
the expected stable groups. The actual replacement adjunctions and family localization give arbitrary
small source products, preserved by the original stable homotopy functors. The actual
local category also has these products, and both categories have constructed
Milnor homotopy limits with their specified triangles. For the fixed source
universe, their original stable homotopy groups have the proved finite-group
and surjective-transition comparisons and lower-bound criteria. Actual source
Milnor limits preserve HF2 locality. The actual scalar-power Moore tower
and its Milnor limit now have their specified unit, proved universal
property and lower-bound preservation; at scalar two the original

on already HF2-local objects. HF2 locality of the required bounded-below
Moore outputs remains a separate obligation. The actual mod-two Moore heart
represents exponent-two classes and has constructed coproduct covers and
relation fibers. Scalar-cofiber and nilpotence arguments supply the finite-power
locality lifting step. The exponent-two layer and bounded-below Moore-output
locality proofs now reduce to the still-unproved original Moore-heart-to-HF2
comparison being an isomorphism. Actual sphere-factor
smash preserves stable equivalences, and space smash commutes with literal
mapping cones at compactly generated levels. The canonical cone-to-quotient
comparison is a stable equivalence for maps with levelwise homotopy extension.
Actual fiber paths now prove literal-cone exactness and stable invariance for
arbitrary original prespectra, giving HEP pushout gluing without object
cofibrancy assumptions. The based version of homotopy extension is proved
for boundary tensors with arbitrary spectra and suffices for the same
original stable gluing. Actual signed loop rows give the diagonal-smash
comparison diagram, its proved row colimit and equal-index cofinal quotient,
now naturally identified with the original diagonal stable groups.
Unrestricted diagonal flatness still requires the rowwise CW result.
Actual parameter smash colimits and cubical cell
sequences are proved with their stated topology and cofibrancy hypotheses.
Finite parameter cell attachments now preserve stable equivalences for
arbitrary original input spectra, including actual finite simplicial realizations
with a disjoint basepoint. Arbitrary wedges now have finite support up to a
relative homotopy, yielding stable invariance for arbitrary strict coproducts
and arbitrary cell families. The infinite sequential CW step remains. Multiplication by two is invertible
on the fixed doubled-bond sphere and all of its specified shifts. Its actual
comparison with the dyadic scalar sphere telescope is now an isomorphism;
transport of the original completeness predicate is still required. The actual
HF2 identity and all its shifts have characteristic two. These results prove
that every HF2-local object is 2-complete; the fixed reflection construction
and the sphere unit's Moore universal property remain unfinished.
The fixed Dold--Kan geometric coefficient spectrum now satisfies
the original source $H\mathbb F_2$ characterization, including genuine stable
homotopy groups, zero, normalized coefficients, and loop addition. Its actual
projection lifting is proved using finite support, open cell-attachment charts,
