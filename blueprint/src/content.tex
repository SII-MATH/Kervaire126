\chapter{Mathematical background}
\label{chap:mathematical-setting}

This chapter fixes the stable homotopy, Steenrod, Ext, Adams-tower, and
convergence language used throughout the formalization, including the general
tower and page constructions used by later chapters. The target-specific
class and its permanence statement are introduced with the final proof rather
than treated as part of the general background. Detailed algebraic and
spectral-sequence foundations and their low-level auxiliary lemmas are retained
in Appendix~\ref{chap:mathematical-foundations-appendix}.

Parameterized constructions retain their stated categorical, comparison, and
convergence hypotheses. Their formalization does not construct the fixed
sphere or synthetic model: the existence and applicability of those instances,
and the literature and computation deliveries on them, remain separate
mathematical obligations.
